  [high] R3 ADR-0020 declared closed by TASK-0091 (373) while 16 event lines remain undrawn and ADR-0020 п.14 (Berlin 4, Vienna 5) is unmet: ADR-0020 R3 asks for the region count 'after all lines' and the list of small sector regions. TASK-0091 explicitly reports that three western Berlin sectors and the Vienna sectors could not be drawn (Berlin = 2 instead of 4, Vienna = 1 instead of 5), and 16 lines are `not_drawn`. ADR-0020 п.14 (ACCEPTED) mandates Berlin 4 and Vienna 5. Therefore 373 is not the post-line count, and the '~27 headroom' over 373 is an unquantified estimate: drawing only Berlin (+2) and Vienna (+4) already adds 6, and further lines (Kashmir, South Sakhalin/Kuriles, etc.) add more. Closing R3 silently retires an accepted ADR requirement and makes the headroom analysis unreliable.
  [high] The mandatory 400-region tick-cost measurement has no EXP/task and no region layer in the synthetic harness: ADR-0022 makes measured cost the only guard for 250→400 growth ('цена тика проверяется замером, а не оценкой'), but names only 'the next full-scale measurement after EXP-0007' and 'R1 ADR-0020'. EXP-0007's frozen pre-registration (П1) builds actors and knowledge nodes only, with no regions; R1 is a research point without an EXP id. A 400-region Lab world needs a region layer (BFS d(r,r') matrix, region columns H/M/K/N_r, region adjacency), i.e. SynthWorldGenerator/SynthWorldSpec changes not scoped anywhere. Without this, the ADR's central mitigation has no execution path and 'до кода E1' cannot be enforced.
  [medium] §1 calls 400 a 'жёсткий порог валидатора' while §3 and the tests implement a warning: §1: 'верх 400 … как жёсткий порог валидатора'. §3: 'регионов > 400 — предупреждение'. Tests: '401 регион — предупреждение, 400 — нет'. A developer cannot tell whether 401 must fail the build or only warn; the ADR also states elsewhere that map reduction must not be silent, which a warning permits. This is an internal contradiction in the normative part.
  [medium] 'Квота великой державы' is undefined and §3 checks any actor, broadening ADR-0011's 'per great power' rule: ADR-0011 §1/§2 п.10 limits '5–15 regions per great power'. ADR-0022 recasts it as core-only, but §2 keeps 'великая держава' and §3 says 'квота метрополии > 15 у актора' — i.e. every actor. No great-power list or definition exists in the linked files. A developer must know who is checked; a non-great but large actor with >15 core regions would be warned, changing the meaning of the original decision without justification.
  [medium] §2 states a 5–15 quota while §3 enforces only the upper 15 and calls the 5 'only world-wide', which is meaningless at 150–400: §2: 'Квота великой державы: 5–15 регионов'. §3: 'квота метрополии > 15 у актора — предупреждение (нижняя граница 5 — только по всему миру)'. World-wide 5 has no meaning when the world minimum is 150. Tests do not cover any lower bound. This is an internal inconsistency and silently drops a constraint from ADR-0011.
  [medium] Acceptance criterion 'no quota warning for GBR, FRA, SUN (если их метрополии ≤ 15)' rests on unverified core counts: The ADR's own verification is conditional on data not provided by TASK-0095 (which reports only colony totals: GBR 85, FRA 45, SUN 31). SUN (USSR) plausibly has ≥15 core regions (union republics/subdivisions); if so the build produces a warning and the stated verification fails, and a legitimate great power is flagged.
