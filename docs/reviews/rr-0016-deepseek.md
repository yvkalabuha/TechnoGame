- [high] Open question 1 and the owner-input list ignore decision #279: DECISIONS_LOG #279 (2026-11-17) already fixes the in-bloc penalty: nominal by default, growing with dependence on the bloc leader if the leader disapproves (Suez). RR-0016 A3 implements exactly that rule but still asks the owner in Q1 whether to «оставить падение поддержки в блоке слабым по умолчанию, но усиливать его экономической зависимостью от патрона». The header lists only #274, #275, #278 and «принятые позже #336, #344, #349–#351, #362, #363, #365, #372»; #279 is absent, so the document re-opens a decided question and understates the owner-input base. #280–#282 are also directly relevant and uncited.
- [high] v1 scope contradicts #282: space warfare, space treaty and UN are in the model while #282 puts them in a second wave: #282 (2026-11-17) fixes War v1 scope as land/sea/air theatres with superiority and landings, ally entry and aid, world support, and peace outcomes including armistice; «Space warfare, treaties and the UN come in a second wave». RR-0016 G2 models a space theatre and an Outer-Space-type treaty as the recommended v1 rule; H2 models the UN/Security Council as a v1 institute; the «Решено предложением по умолчанию» block states «космический бой допустим до договора, договор — по условиям (G2); ООН с 1945 — сценарное событие». At the same time Q4's proposed answer says «космос и миротворцы — FUTURE». The document is internally inconsistent and contradicts #282. H2 also calls the UN a 1945 scripted event, while #281 makes it start data for 1945 and condition-based for later campaigns.
- [high] Tick-budget claim is off by ~30×: per-day war cost compared with the monthly reserve: ADR-0016 §1/§3 sets a monthly budget (p95 ≤500 ms/month, reserve 350 ms/month); ADR-0020 п.20 adds provinces ≤10 ms from the same reserve. RR-0016 explicitly moves the estimate to a day («теперь на тик (день)»): 0.3–3 ms/day, p95 ≤5 ms/day, then claims «≈ 1,4% резерва 350 мс» (5/350). A 5 ms/day p95 is roughly 100–150 ms per month (29–43% of the reserve), and 0.3–3 ms/day is roughly 9–90 ms/month (2.6–26%), before BFS and ally/aid updates. The estimate also says BFS runs «только при изменении сети», but the network changes whenever controller changes during an offensive, i.e. almost daily for active wars (10⁴ × active wars).
- [high] New stocks/entities and inputs are not proposed to RR-0011; owner/writer/hash/save are undefined: RR-0011 §1.3/§2.1 states the convention: a new RR does not introduce its own entities, keys or stocks; if something is missing it proposes a row to the map. RR-0016 introduces S_world/S_bloc, War/Front, join, aid_level/aid_pool, advance_acc, Frozen{line,buffer,breach_counter}, prisoners, res, mob, terrain, fort, defender_force, supply_head, and a «пакт» input for B2, but proposes no E:/A:/D: rows, no owner system for S_world/S_bloc/join/aid_level, and no hash/save/one-writer declaration for them. The only writer statement covers War/Front and territory. This leaves determinism (hash), save format and one-writer ownership undefined, contrary to ADR-0019 п.4 and RR-0011.

RR-0016 introduces S_world/S_bloc and says «S_world — внешняя величина; внутренняя legit — отдельная (#363)», but never reconciles S_world with `support`/`state_capacity`. C2's «через владельца поддержки» also leaves the writer ambiguous.
