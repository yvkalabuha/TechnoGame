1. (high) A1, fixed goal weights ×3/×2/×1.5. [Факт, KnowledgeSystem.cs:243, ComputeFull] A node's money = its weight / the sum of weights over the whole frontier; weight = prio (1 or 2, PolicyRules.Priority) × J. So the share that goal 1 gets depends on how many nodes are in the frontier: 3/(N+2) gives about 14–25 % at N = 10–20, not the 35–55 % the doc claims. The figure drifts by year and by country, so the player cannot predict it. Alternative (strong): one player slider "доля науки на цели" (0–80 %), split among goals 50/30/20, the rest by the usual weights.
