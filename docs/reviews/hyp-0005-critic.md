# Gate HYP-0005 — critic (2026-11-18)

P1–P3 are decoupled correctly; Pocock level 0.0294 is right. Main problem: the outcome rule cannot give `refutes` for "predetermined" (a HYP-0002 repeat). Also undefined formulas at t0 and free parameters of the start perturbation and correction. The file has no numbered author questions 1–10 (only a gate question on P3, l. 122); answers below follow the 10 task criteria.

- **H1 (outcome rule, l. 50–51, 61–63).** At chg30 < 0.10 there are < 30 changes, P2 is replaced by `mob5`, which measures reshuffles of places 2–5 — #329 will almost surely produce them, so P2 "passes", `refutes` (P1 fail AND P2 fail) is unreachable, the outcome is `inconclusive`, K7 does not close. "Random" is almost unrefutable too (P1 fail-high also needs Neff_c > 5 with ~8 great powers). Fix: `refutes` = P1 fail (either side) OR P3 fail OR (P2 estimable AND P2 fail); `mob5` diagnostic. Changes the rule of the approved plan (l. 60, 75) → owner.
- **H2 (race object, l. 28–30).** At t0 = 1945-10 axis A ("12-month sum") and the tie rule ("mean R over 12 months") are undefined — no history, so L(t0), the P1 base, is undefined. L(t0) has no hysteresis: a near-tie at start gives a "change" in the first 12 months and inflates chg30. Fix: A(t0) from the annual output of the start table; warm-up — L(t0) = record leader over 1945-10…1946-09, or changes before t0+12 not counted; print the share of seeds tied at t0.
- **H3 (l. 76, 107, 100).** Free parameters: (a) perturbation level unspecified — independent regional draws shrink national spread ~1/√k and bring the floor back; must be per source estimate (#329): national total and proxy shares separately; (b) distribution shape deferred to the manifest; (c) the content of the "one correction" is chosen after seeing the pilot. Fix: fix level, shape (truncated normal σ = half-interval/2, truncated at the interval; or uniform) and independence in the HYP; pre-define the correction as one action (e.g. "distribution → uniform over the whole interval").
- **M1 (pilot, l. 106).** With constant L(t0), "share of one leader at t1" ≈ 1 − chg30 — the pilot leaks chg30. Fix: print only the ≥ 90 % flag and the L(t0) distribution; seal the share by hash until 3.5. A second binary floor check at the 3.3 freeze, without correction rights (the broad event list loads after the pilot).
- **M2 (sequential plan, l. 93).** (a) P2 metric may switch between steps (< 30 changes at 300, > 30 at 600) — fix P2 at the 600 step; (b) unclear whether `mob5` enters the extension rule; (c) half-widths given for 95 % (0.052, 0.037), at 97.1 % they are 0.058, 0.041; (d) pass by point: Pocock does not control false `supports` — record as a deliberate asymmetry (#323).
- **M3 (l. 100, 131).** `knife_edge` in "success criteria" but not in the rule; the scale parameter "assigned at calibration" is chosen later; ×1.5 exceeds #329. Fix: `knife_edge` diagnostic; name the parameter and grid now; mark ×1.5 outside #329.
- **M4 (G(t), l. 27).** "≥ 5 regions" depends on region slicing (item 1.1, not ready) — a degree of freedom; S6 nearly empty by construction. Undefined: record leader drops out of G; joint polity shares vs the 5 threshold. Fix: region table hash in the manifest before the pilot; print G(t0); "leader outside G ⇒ change to the current month leader, no window"; great powers from the reference point, not per seed.
- **M5 (S2/K8, l. 98).** Undefined: the "neutralise the cause" operator per why_not type, the "change disappeared" criterion (±12 months, same challenger?), the sampling rule for 30 changes. The 70 % threshold can be tuned otherwise.
