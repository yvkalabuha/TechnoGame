# Gate RR-0016 (revised) — critic (2026-11-18)

War is built on economy, logistics and technology, no tactical game. 4 high: tick-budget unit error; taint boundary unresolved; the "wars replace war_related" contract is unreachable; no war-start model. 6 medium, 3 low.

- **HIGH §Determinism, cost.** Units mixed: the 350 ms reserve of ADR-0016 is per game month (l. 30, 100), so 5 ms/tick (day) × 30 = 150 ms/month ≈ 43 % of the reserve, not 1.4 %. Provinces ≤ 10 ms are also per month (ADR-0020 p. 20). 0.3–3 ms/day for war alone exceeds ≈ 2.7 ms/day for all systems (RR-0011 §6, quoted in RR-0019). Fix: everything in ms/month against the reserve left after E1, provinces, logistics B and ADR-0021; «Проверки M2» p. 5 in ms/month; if it does not fit, combat every N days or degradation gates.
- **HIGH §Согласование, «Разногласия» 1.** Variant (c) solves nothing: ADR-0021 p. 17 taints `provinces.controller` and bans `controls`, not only `at_war`/`war_over`. Why tainted: F2 takes `supply_mult` from flows, flows read `link.failure` — so `War` is tainted even without its own randomness; variant (b) fails too. Peaceful base events use `not at_war` in S (FRG 1949, Austria 1955) and `controls` in N for all transfers (#339). Loophole: p. 16a(г) exempts `ScenarioEventSystem` from the transitive rule; if it applies the war journal, taint is "laundered". Fix: a systems-architect question for all M2 base events, options: (1) base events become branches after the first M2 war; (2) a separate untainted "historical" ownership layer; (3) owner revision of #342/#366. Extend the disagreement to `controls` and name the 16a(г) loophole.
- **HIGH §Согласование, summary p. 4.** (a) The `war_related` list marked [Факт] is wrong: #336 = Algeria, Indochina 1954, Palestine 1948, Indonesia 1949, Goa 1961; India's partition, FRG, Austria, Taiwan, Oder–Neisse are not `war_related` (in the document only Algeria carries the flag). (b) Almost all #336 cases are independence or insurgent wars where one side is not yet a polity; `War{aggressor, sides}` covers only interstate war, so the #336 replacement is impossible. Fix: correct the list; add a movement side (`create_polity` at war start or an insurgent actor) or keep these transfers scripted and ask the owner to refine #336.
- **HIGH §B, §I, §J.** No war-start model: who declares and under what condition — only allied entry exists. War frequency undefined, M2 criteria 1–2 untestable. Fix: a `declare` formula in the weekly slot (#344) (target, force estimate, `T`, `S_world`, deterrence RR-0013) with a reason journal; calibration over many seeds.
- MED §F2 vs RR-0019 B: flow assignment recomputes per window (week/month) on a `util` snapshot; a controller change in merge D−1 resets Φ and `util` (RR-0019 l. 86); every front day is a new controller change → daily Φ and assignment recompute, outside the RR-0019 budget; encirclement only in the next window. Military CMP classes (ammunition/fuel) are missing from the E2 minimum. Fix: a fixed assignment window for fronts, batch controller changes until the window, name military CMP classes in E2 scope.
- MED §F: no force variables (strength, losses, replacement from fleet and production, manpower); `stalemate = f(…losses…)`, prisoners and "budget race" rest on undefined terms. Fix: minimal `force[side, front]` and a loss formula in Fx6.
- MED §B2: deciding entry "in merge D−1" violates ADR-0019 (AI decisions in `Decision`; merge D−1 only applies dated events). Fix: compute in `Decision`, apply in merge.
- MED §Determinism: "`entity_id` = war id from data" is wrong — wars are created in play; per ADR-0021 p. 8 a counter `sid` with preallocated `max_ids`; >20 wars undefined; war streams (`war.surprise`, `war.weather`, aid exposure) not listed. Fix: a stream table and an overflow rule.
- MED §Проверки M2: thresholds 60 % and 15 % are arbitrary (tuning risk); population undefined (scenario, horizon, all wars or player's); the key claim (economy and logistics decide outcomes) is not measured. Fix: (a) L1/L2 with war at threads 1/4/8; (b) `--stochastic off` — all wars end; (c) monotonic outcome in production and supply ratio, ablation of `supply_mult`; (d) non-degenerate outcome spread across seeds.
- MED, history: the table is from memory, #321 needs FINAL. Errors: US advisers in Indochina before Tonkin (1950s); PSO test list lacks India 2019; Israel withdrew only in March 1957. Fix: research-scientist verifies with primary sources before FINAL; DRAFT until then.
- LOW §I: DMZ as "buffer region state" takes a whole region under strict regions (ADR-0020 p. 9) → DMZ as a front-edge attribute.
- LOW §D: technology enters combat only via "generation quality" → bind `mob`, quality and range explicitly to Tech nodes, science at the centre.
- LOW «Разногласия» 2–3: both compatible with the RR-0019 M2 slice (blockade FUTURE, airfields M3) — a scope choice; close 2 as "blockade = closing a sea link via sea gates", keep 3 as question 4.

Open: `war_related` and insurgent sides may need an owner refinement of #336; the taint boundary is for systems-architect, possibly with an owner revision of #342/#366.
