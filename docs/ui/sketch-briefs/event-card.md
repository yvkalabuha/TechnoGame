# Sketch brief: event card

Redesign the attached in-game screen. A decision sheet ("event card") over the main game screen, opened from the Politics area. Russian UI text. Output: one 16:9 mockup image, 1920x1080 composition.

Style: paper map, cream paper, ink engraving, drafting table under a lamp (owner decisions: paper; engraving in ink). Clear type scale, tables instead of phrases, one button set, colour never the only carrier of meaning. The sheet is 1040x640 on a dimmed map, with a binding edge and shadow like the lever windows.

Layout:
- Header strip: a small flag plate (3:2, ink outline) and the title in one line: "Франция, 1953. Расходы на войну в Индокитае". Top right: "Закрыть (Esc)".
- Left column: situation text (3 sentences), a line "Появилось, потому что: ...", and a block "Что дальше" with two short rows for 6 and 12 months.
- Right: a comparison table of 3 options as 3 equal columns. Each column has the same four rows: title with a square mark, price lines (money, relations, intelligence, war risk in words), condition ("доступен" or "закрыт: ..."), a button "Выбрать". One column is selected: a thick blue frame, a filled dot and the word "выбрано". One column is closed: hatching, a lock and the reason line above its button.
- Footer: left, a summary line "Изменится: ..."; right, one main button "Принять вариант" and two secondary buttons "Избежать" (with its cost and the counter "избегано 1 из 2") and "Отложить на…".
- A small warning plate with a word, not only colour: "Внимание: следующее «избежать» будет последним".

Constraints:
- Flags are small plates with a 1 px ink outline, always left of a country name; a flag is never the only carrier of meaning. Draw simple stripes only, no emblems, no real coat of arms.
- No text baked into textures that is not Russian UI text; no logos; no internal ids on screen.
- Body text not smaller than 13 px at 1920x1080, numbers always with a unit. Decoration quieter than data.
