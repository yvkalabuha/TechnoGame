# Sketch brief: levers-windows

Four in-game windows of a 1945–2100 science and technology strategy game. Reference layouts and text are in docs/ui/specs/levers-windows.md. Output: four 16:9 mockup images, 1920x1080 composition, one per window. Russian UI text.

Style (owner decisions): paper map, cream paper, ink engraving, drafting-sheet language, thin crisp lines like engineering drawings, GOST drafting lettering for headings. Windows are one sheet 1600x880 centred over a darkened world globe, same frame for all four: header 64 px with a serif title, four tab chips (Бюджет, Институты, Производство, Рынок лицензий; active one filled), a "× Закрыть" button at the top right; footer 72 px with a summary line on the left and one main button on the right. Not a dark neon dashboard, not a sci-fi HUD, no glass, no glow, no gradients.

Rules for every image: type at least 13 px at 1920x1080, numbers always with units next to every gauge; colour never the only carrier of meaning (use hatching, shapes, words); disabled buttons show hatching, a lock and a reason sentence under them; one main action per window. Show text as real readable Russian, but it will be drawn by code later, so exact words are not binding. Money looks like "$31 млрд (в ценах 1990)".

Window 1: Бюджет державы. Header numbers: "$31 млрд (в ценах 1990)", "растёт на 2,1 %/год", reserve "На счёте: $2,4 млрд". A horizontal stacked bar split into four hatched segments with percent inside. Under it four rows, each: name, one sentence of effect, a slider track with 25 % ticks and a 24x32 handle, then amount "$11,8 млрд" and "38 %", and − + buttons. One row (Освоение технологий) is below a marked 10 % threshold and shows a warning plate with a triangle, hatching and the word "стоп". Right column: reserve card with a 3-line table (1, 3, 5 years), button "Ускоренная программа…", a short decision log. Footer: "Изменится со следующего месяца: Наука +$0,3 млрд" and button "Применить".

Window 2: Институты. Left: six table rows 104 px high, each with a 72 px emblem on the left, name and "США · основан 1915", five small area icons (aerodynamics wing profile, propulsion turbine disc, materials ingot, chemistry retort, mechanics gear) each with a level digit 1–4 and four dots, and a priority cell: target node name, "металлы · бонус +15 %", a progress bar with "32 %"; one row "Свободен" with a button "Выбрать узел…"; one row under construction with dashed outline emblem. Emblems are ORIGINAL badges: silhouette by country (shield with flat top for USA, double-ring circle for Britain, octagon for USSR, rounded lozenge for France, notched square for Sweden), an engraved area glyph in the centre, two or three initials, a small year ribbon; never copy or resemble real agency logos (no NASA, DARPA, ONERA marks, no stars, eagles or red-white-blue). Right: list "Построить институт" with four candidate rows and a table card "Что даёт / Цена / Чем платим / Что изменится", two payment toggles, a disabled button "Начать строительство" with its reason under it.

Window 3: Линия производства. Left: caption "Тип: реактивный истребитель", note "Пока доступны только реактивные истребители", list of models with stage badges (word plus shape). Centre: model card, a three-step strip (Проект, Прототип, Серия) with filled, half and empty markers and months left, a field "Скорость выпуска, машин/мес." with a number, an optional "Заказ N машин" field, a small table with units (себестоимость $ млн за машину, освоение процесса %, в строю машин), buttons "Запустить серию" and a quieter "Запустить досрочно…", a quiet "Закрыть линию". Right: "Что тормозит" list, the alloy shown as an ingot icon with a stock bar and "хватает на 62 % выпуска" (not a flask), plus a fleet table. Bottom strip: up to three adviser cards "Совет: открыть линию …" each with reason, cost and two buttons "Принять" and "Отказать".

Window 4: Рынок лицензий. Two tabs "Купить" and "Продать". Buy: left list of sellers with state tint chips and counts; centre catalogue table with columns product, seller price, a short price scale (marker at seller price, tick at reference price, diamond at world average, with number "+30 %" and ▲), reference, average, "Купить"; right a table card with what the licence gives, price, payment from reserve, project slot used, a main button and a reason line when blocked. Below: "У вас уже есть" row.

Improve what you can in hierarchy and rhythm, but keep the structure. Avoid: generic SaaS dashboards, rounded pill buttons, drop shadows on every card, decorative charts, gauges without numbers, dark UI.
