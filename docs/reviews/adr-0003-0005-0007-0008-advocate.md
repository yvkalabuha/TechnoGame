# Gate ADR-0003 / 0005 / 0007 / 0008 rev 2 — advocate (2026-11-18)

Two factual mismatches with owner decisions and code; three strong alternatives (slot reuse with generation, sum-preserving rounding instead of bare floor, ban on culture-dependent string comparisons). Other rejected options (Int128, own Roslyn analyzer, Parquet now, ECS) lose — keep as is.

## ADR-0003
- **strong, fact:** p. 3 says money in "millions of 1990 dollars" — contradicts #368 (millions of $ 2011, Maddison). Fix p. 3, close question 1 on money.
- **strong, fact:** question 2 on R4 is decided by #375 (ToyFunnel to Lab, R4 exception removed); reflect in p. 7 and «Открыто».
- **strong — sum-preserving rounding vs bare floor.** Splitting a flow among N receivers with floor loses up to N−1 raw per tick; over 190 years an unexplained leak of money and population. Rule: conserved quantities are distributed via `DetMath.Allocate` (largest remainders, sum exactly equals input) plus a conservation invariant test.
- moderate — per-quantity scales rightly rejected; the real risk of one Fx6 is unit mixing (M$ vs $, TWh vs GWh). Cheap fix: unit in column metadata (`unit:` at registration) and a check at data load; `Money`/`Energy` wrapper types later if errors appear.
- weak — Int128 for money: with "M$" the headroom is huge (~10¹¹ over the horizon vs 9.2·10¹²); Int128 doubles memory and changes hash/snapshot format. Keep; a range test is enough.

## ADR-0005 (gate together with ADR-0020, #374)
- **strong — slot reuse by lowest free index with a generation column.** "Never reused" with `max_designs: 12` (mods/core/scenario_1945.yaml:22) over 1945–2100 will hit the ceiling, and "refuse with why_not" (#374) becomes a permanent dead end. Reuse with generation stays deterministic and compatible with the ADR-0013 snapshot; references check (index, generation). Owner decision: softens #374 without reversing it.
- **strong — tables per entity type vs slot groups:** columns grow as actors × slots × fields; do not fix p. 8 "one table" as a decision — state it as the M0 transitional state with a reference to ADR-0020.
- weak — dynamic `RowCount` and ECS: keep.

## ADR-0007
- **strong, test gap:** `DeterminismRules` misses culture-dependent string comparisons (`SortedDictionary<string,…>`/`OrderBy(s)` without a comparer, `string.CompareTo`, `Comparer<string>.Default`); order depends on CurrentCulture and ICU/NLS and can differ across OSes. Add to p. 6 and the test.
- fact: `string.GetHashCode` is listed as a gap but `DeterminismRules.cs` already catches it (l. 161–164).
- moderate — instead of an own Roslyn analyzer, `Microsoft.CodeAnalysis.BannedApiAnalyzers` with `BannedSymbols.txt` (IDE errors at build) as a complement to the IL test; needs an update of `CoreProject_PackageReferences_AreExactlyApproved`. Optional, light.
- moderate — `Array.Sort`/`List.Sort` are unstable on equal keys; safe for L1/L2, a risk for L3 and runtime changes. Rule: sort keys are unique or have an explicit tie-break.

## ADR-0008 (stack fixed by #374)
- weak — Parquet/DuckDB now: the streaming loader closed #264; Parquet breaks "stdlib + script hash". Keep.
- moderate — the 10 GB / 30 min trigger is not observable; write total CSV size into `matrix.json` and analysis duration into the experiment log.
- weak — golden matrix in CI: `selftest` already compares 1 and 4 threads. Keep.

Open: text fixes (systems-architect: #368, #375 in ADR-0003; p. 6 in ADR-0007); culture-comparison test (developer, light); slot reuse with generation (owner, softens #374).
