# Sketch brief: knowledge tree, year = distance from centre (decision #473)

Two 16:9 mockups, 1920x1080, Russian UI text, for the game screen "Дерево знаний" of a 1945–2100 science and technology strategy game. Reference only: the real screen is drawn by code, so do not rely on pixel details.

## Keep from the earlier sketches (owner liked them)
- "Atlas" colour: warm cream paper, ink lines, soft watercolour wash per sector (three to five calm hues), labels along the rim, small neighbour call-outs on plates.
- "Folio" inspector card on the right (about 40 % of width): header with a status plate, a recessed picture window with an ink engraving, two tidy zebra tables, the reason written under a disabled button.

## New layout (the main change)
- Circular map. **Distance from the centre = year.** Centre = 1945. Nine concentric rings, one per decade, labelled 1950, 1960, 1970, 1980, 1990, 2000, 2010, 2020, 2030 along the upward vertical line; 1980 and 2000 slightly thicker.
- **Radial sectors = research directions** (five now; the design must take 6–10 later). Each sector has its own wash and its own hatch angle (colour is not the only carrier) and a name on the outer rim.
- A thin dashed ring "Сейчас: 1953" marks the current year; nodes outside it are lighter. Centre: a small mark with the text "1945 · корни".
- Nodes: six shapes (circle, square, triangle, diamond, hexagon, pentagon) for node types; filled = known, outlined = can be researched now, dashed = locked. About 70 nodes, denser near inner rings; every node visible as a dot even at overview, so the tree feels large. Header line: "Узлов: 74 · открыто 23".
- Bottom-left of the map: square buttons "+", "−", "Весь диск" and the text "Шаг 3 из 6". Search field above the map: "Найти узел…".

## Mockup 1: overview with the card
Whole disc, one selected node on the 1960 ring with a double ring, the route to a goal as a thick blue line. Right card for "Треугольное крыло": status plate "Известно"; picture window (ink engraving of a delta wing); table "Что даёт": "Скорость +30 км/ч", "Манёвр −10", "Дальность +5" (sign and unit in every row); table "Зависимости и открытия"; sections "Цель" and "Фокус освоения", each with one explanatory line; a disabled button "Отдать приказ: фокус" with the reason under it: "Нечего отдавать: не выбран ни один процесс". A visible button "× Закрыть" in the top-right corner of the sheet.

## Mockup 2: maximum zoom with a radial menu
A few nodes of one sector, large. Each node is a pictogram (wing, engine, radar) inside its shape frame, the name under it, the year, and two or three effect chips such as "скорость +30", "ночные операции +10". Over one node a radial menu of four round buttons, each with a word: "Сделать целью", "В фокус освоения" (hatched; centre text: "Нужен известный процесс"), "Что мешает", "Показать связи".

## Constraints
- Cream paper, ink engraving; no dark theme, no neon, no sci-fi glow. Black text on cream, at least 13 px at 1920x1080; no tiny decorative lettering.
- Only the Russian texts quoted above; no other invented numbers or labels; no lorem ipsum.
- No colour-only meaning: statuses also by shape, hatch or word.
- Output: two images, 1920x1080 each.
