FINDINGS:
1. high, §6 and the Краткий вывод. The claim that the leader will discover new nodes until about 2010 has no calculation. The TM-2030 measurement points the other way (m-play-horizon-2030.md «После 1977»). USA knew all 61 nodes by 1970 on a budget of 2.6. In 2000–2030, 94–98 % of tooling money stayed unspent. By 1990–2010 the budget is 3.8–7. So 16 nodes with cost multipliers ×6.4 and ×10.2 will probably all be discovered by about 1985–1995, and 1995–2030 stays empty. The real bottleneck is getting aircraft into service: G = 0 for decades, and only USA has G4 in service by 2030. G5/G6 add a costlier af_class 5 and more processes, so a G5 in service before 2030 is doubtful. Fix: before the gate, simulation-engineer estimates months per node from CostN and the research budget for USA/SUN/FRA in 1975/1990/2010. Then a data-only probe on a branch, with thresholds written down in advance: the leader's last new node ≥ 2005; at least 2 countries have G5 in service by 2030; the narrow dead-time metric for 1978–2030 falls below 0.8. If a threshold fails, the gate holds.
2. high, §1 and §5 (questions 2, 3). Catch-up choice: each generation is one AND chain, there is no `any_of`, and all 16 nodes have `hazard: none`. Discovery order is then set by budget alone. The player has nothing to choose except "research everything", and the TM-2030 frozen leadership (USA wins 5/5) is reinforced. This goes against #422 (the goal is to catch up). Fix: minimal branching. G5 gets `platform_min_rank` 5, so the composite airframe becomes a perf-versus-cost choice. Or G5 takes `{any_of: [composite_airframe, fly_by_wire]}`. Record which choice the player gets as an explicit criterion for game-designer.
3. medium, §1 «hazard: none». The reason given is that the L_K/L_K0 control layers fail on G5 (H9). That is a Lab problem. The FULL model doesn't need those tables, and the guard H8 (control layer + ticks > 384 → error) is already in the plan (note §5, T-H1 p.7). Fix: keep `hz` on the two FND nodes 62 and 63 in FULL, and reject control layers on data with gen > 4 through the guard. Variant T is not needed. Not verified: whether `hz` in FULL depends on per-generation tables.
4. medium, anchors G6 and the G6 window 1962–2010. The G6 APP anchor is the 1990 prototype first flight; the last anchor in the whole content is 1990. So by design the content has no history after 1990, against the 2030 horizon (#423). Fix: give G6 an in-service anchor (F-22 IOC 2005 [Факт, Code One S-I]). Or put cost multipliers and anchors into the calibration criterion from point 1. If the probe still shows dead time, add one late node (sensors/AESA, about 2000–2005). That last part needs a D1 decision from the owner, because it is new scope.
5. medium, sources (S-A, S-C, S-F, S-G, S-J). Only one source page was opened in full. Six anchors (64, 66, 72–75) are estimates or memory. NASA and BAE pages returned 404. Fix: use primary PD sources (NASA NTRS technical reports on F-8 DFBW and DEEC, the af.mil fact sheet). For the G5 APP anchor, prefer slice countries: Rafale A, FRA, 4 July 1986, or Su-27, SUN, 1977, instead of the GBR demonstrator EAP. Settle 1962 vs 1964 for node 63.
6. medium, §1 nodes 63 and 67 against G4. Node 63 (gen 6, anchor 1962) is a 1962 theory priced at the ×10.2 G6 rate. Node 67 is anchored on YF-16 (1974), which is the same historical generation as the G4 APP (1972). The cost multiplier by generation pushes these early discoveries late. Fix: node 63 → gen 5 (or mark it explicitly as "theory priced by the generation where it is used"). Explain in the item why FBW is G5 and not G4.
7. medium, §6 against #431/#435–#437 (fleet ageing). The hypothesis "catch-up countries overtake by generation in service" ignores ageing with a 5-year half-life and the retire-without-replacement lever. A country that switches its line to G5 and does not reach a series loses its effective units and its rank. Fix: add to the T-M criteria: years with G = 0 per country, and the share of time the in-service test (50 effective units) fails in 1990–2030.
8. medium, §4 items 7 and 8. Item 7 can be checked now [Факт, ProductsSystem.cs:107-108]: in `CanonicalLp` mode `LpTDesign.Length != layout.Generations` throws. An L_P run on 6-generation data fails even at 388 months, and the H8 guard (ticks > 384) does not catch it. Fix: extend the guard to "control layer + gen > 4", or require 6 entries in the manifests. Item 8 is already settled as a fact in the note §3 (`ProductClassTable.Build`); remove "Проверить" there. `SalePhi` caps any gap ≥ 3 at 0.3, so G6 vs G1 gives the same discount as G4 vs G1; record this as a deliberate choice.
9. low, §2 airframe rank 7. `perf_mult` 2.1 for stealth is a placeholder, and the document says so. To avoid a fake mechanic, the gate should record that the stealth airframe is only an entry requirement for G6, not a stealth model, and list it in Разногласия/§12.
10. low, §2 «Другие данные» and `pu_ranks`/`af_class 5`. `max_designs` 12→16 has no justification: the probe used 4 of 12 slots. `proc_scale_pu` 1.2 and `proc_scale_af` 4.2 are labelled stubs. Fix: keep 12 until the probe shows the slots fill up. Compute `proc_scale` by rules R-PS2m and R-HF before the data commit, not after.
11. low, §1 node 65. The composite design node requires `s5.cmp.aluminium_alloy_sheet`. The real base of boron-epoxy is fibre and resin chemistry, and no node for it exists. Accept this as a simplification and record it in the item.
