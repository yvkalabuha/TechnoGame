# Gate review: docs/game-design/notes/production-mastery.md — critic

> 2026-11-24, owner decision #439. Report copied verbatim from the agent.

STATUS: done
SUMMARY: Nothing makes the draft unusable as a whole, but it has two high-severity defects. The programme's "Mastered" stage cannot be reached, because the programme produces no output. The fix for failure (В) is mixed in with the programme, although the evidence says they are separable. Not checked: IndustrySystem.cs:646; a numeric run of the growth rate at c_p≈0.22; how WhyNot/ToolingPlan use DHForecast.
FILES: none (read-only review)
FINDINGS:
1. **high**, §2 p.3–4 and §3 "Старт серии". Mastered is defined as `yield ≥ 0,7` plus `proc_v ≥ v_min` for 6 months (as in IndustrySystem.cs:646). But the programme "не создаёт выпуска (`v`)". So Mastered is unreachable inside the programme, and the handover (T_hand after Mastered) never triggers. The start condition in §3 (`y_min ≥ 0,5` and Pilot) half-works around this, but it contradicts the state machine in §2. Fix: the programme ends at "ready for series" (`y_min ≥ y_start` in every dimension). From then on, mastery is reached only by output, under the existing rule. Drop the Mastered and Handover stages from the programme, or define them as stages of the series.
2. **high**, Краткий вывод and §7 P6. The probe (§7.4, §7.6 item 2) shows that (В) is fixed by the AI rule alone (Requirement on a generation the actor can master, and closing a candidate line with `y_min < y_prod` after `T_Ab`). On the 61-node build this gives USA G=4 in 8/10 seeds. CF-1 shows that G5 enters service through 52/80 (`hot_sc_cmc`) without (Б). (Б) blocks only 43/73, i.e. the `hot_vim_ds_cooled`/`hot_sc_sc` chains. The draft ties foresight to `Masterable`, and `Masterable` depends on the programme (§3 (в) `V_OC = x_prog`). So foresight cannot ship or be measured without the programme. Fix: smallest version for M-play = #438 + probe fix 2, with `Masterable` = "no `Saturation`/`LabCeiling` limiter at `Hcap(t+T_look)`", no forecast. The programme comes second, and only if the probe shows that 43/73 block G5/G6 in service.
3. **medium-high**, §8 q.1 and §2. The programme is literally "опытный участок" with a nominal output, i.e. the "опытное производство" that #127 rejected. The draft's argument that "the formula does not change, only the input" does not hold: #127 rejected exactly an input variant, not the formula. Raising it to D1 is right. But the draft hides one fact: the I-OC mechanism (`GrowthNominal`, contract r12, CbCultureRule.cs:142) already exists, so the precedent of a nominal output without real output has already been accepted after #127. Fix: give the owner a factual question. "#127 was about the start data. Do you allow extending the existing training nominal (I-OC) to a paid programme?" Option A: yes. Option B: no, then only foresight, and (Б) stays for 43/73.
4. **medium**, §2 p.7 and §8 q.2. The text says "не проверено кодом". In fact `GrowthNominal`/`Planned` already take (actor, process number) through `world.Products` (IndustryInputsAdapter.cs:126–133). So the programme slot fits in ProductsSystem without changing the Core rule. But this is a change to contract v2.4 and a golden re-record. Fix: write this in the draft and remove the assumption.
5. **medium-high**, §3 "Мерцания нет". `Masterable` is a forecast. It depends on H, on the D share and on the queue of slots, not only on `Hcap(t)`. It can flip from year to year, and then Requirement jumps between generations (the A40 repeat-once rule complicates this further). P1 ("never starts what cannot be mastered") checks a forecast against itself, which makes it a tautology. Fix: hysteresis (once Requirement is issued for generation g, it is not lowered while the programme is in Pilot). Add an invariant on the fact: the share of launched series that reach Mastered within N years.
6. **medium**, §2 p.1 and §3. G5 needs 4 tier-5 processes (52, 73, 75, 80), but there are only 2 slots. `Masterable` with `T_look` = 60 does not model the queue, so the forecast is optimistic. Fix: either count the queue explicitly in the forecast, or set slots = the number of unmastered processes of one project.
7. **medium**, §2 p.2(в) and the summary. "No new budget share" contradicts `B_prog` = 25 % of D. The auto-focus of D takes `EffortFocus` slots from other processes, a hidden cost that the player does not see. Fix: show the displaced focus in the programme card; fix one wording.
8. **medium**, §7 and §8 q.4. #101 requires the K2b′ criterion (ΣE ≥ k× the norm and T ≥ m× the norm), with k and m fixed before the run. P5 has only "≤ 25 %", and `upkeep_mult` has no value. Fix: add P-K2b′ with k and m in the pre-registration, and a failure criterion "the programme is cheaper than mastery through output".
9. **medium-high**, §2 and §7. The programme and `Masterable` apply to all processes. The AI will start programmes for G2–G4 too and shift the calibrated history of 1945–1977 (the goldens and the G1–G4 anchors). Fix: allow the programme only for a process whose `limiter₀ = LabCeiling`, or only for tier ≥ 5 in M-play. Add a regression metric for G1–G4 (entry into service, anchors) to the plan.
10. **medium**, §2 and §7 (calibration). There are 7 new constants (`x_prog`, `T_look`, `T_hand`, `T_max`, `B_prog`, slots, `upkeep_mult`) on top of the #438 coefficient, and 10 seeds cannot separate their effects. Fix: derive `T_look` from the `T_Ab`/ageing horizon. Remove `T_hand` (see 1). Calibrate only `x_prog` and `upkeep_mult`.
11. **low**, §4. The progress bar `(H − H₀)/(r_d − H₀)` is not defined for several dimensions. Fix: show the limiting dimension (min) and name it.
12. **low**, §7 P9. "4 of 5 observers" is not feasible for a solo project. Fix: one owner session, with a check-list of causes named without F3.
13. **low**, §3 "Requirement". The text says the newest generation is chosen in two places, but in code `NewestGeneration` is called from both `RequirementVariants` (UI/why_not) and `Decide` (PolicySystem.Products.cs). Fix: change both, or the UI will show a Requirement the AI does not follow.
